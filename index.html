What is Harriton HOSA?

HOSA, a.k.a Future Health Professionals, is an international student-led organization recognized by the U.S. Department of Education and several other federal and state agencies. HOSA’s mission is to empower our Future Health Professionals to become leaders in the global health community and explore their interest in Health Occupations!

HOSA provides a unique program of leadership and offers essential Health Science Education, especially critical when considering the shortage of qualified workers for the health care industry.

Every year, HOSA has a PA State Conference and an International Conference for you to have a chance to show your growth!

SPIRIT NOTE: If you want to be considered for leadership and want priority when signing up for activities and competitive events, show up and show out at meetings and fundraisers!

About Us

At Harriton HOSA we will have speakers come from all around the medical field, service opportunities such as hospital volunteering, fundraising opportunities, and a chance to compete at the state and national level for HOSA.

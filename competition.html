What are SLC and ILC?

SLC — State Leadership Conference — is the Pennsylvania state competition, where Harriton HOSA members compete against other Pennsylvania chapters.

ILC — International Leadership Conference — is HOSA’s international conference, where top finishers from state conferences compete, attend workshops and general sessions, and are recognized for their work.

To qualify for nationals (ILC), you must receive a top placement at States.
